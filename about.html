THE STORY

Why we built this

We use GPS every day, but the technology behind the blue dot is often invisible.

The problem

GPS relies on satellite timing, mathematical positioning and route-finding algorithms. These ideas can be hard to picture from text alone.

Our solution

Lost in the Signal turns these ideas into three interactive experiments. Activate satellites, change a timing measurement and navigate a fictional city. See what changes, then explore why.

How we built it

HTML structures our pages, CSS creates the visual design, JavaScript runs the calculations and Three.js renders optional 3D scenes. The underlying GPS and navigation models remain deliberately simplified for learning.

What you'll discover

GPS positioning, signal accuracy and route planning are connected but distinct technologies. Our project separates them so visitors can explore each step.
