Break the Signal

Why does time matter?

Distance = speed × time. Light travels about 0.30 metres in one nanosecond, so a 100 ns delay adds about 30 metres to one measured distance. That does not necessarily mean the final position is wrong by 30 metres.

Our classroom map uses scaled distances so that the error is visible. Real GPS also needs to correct receiver clock error and other effects.
